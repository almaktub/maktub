About

Who's behind the writing

My name is Yaya. I was born in Pakistan to Afghan parents, moved to Kabul when I was eight, and landed in San Diego at twenty. Farsi at home, Urdu with my grandparents, English everywhere else. I've been an old soul the whole way through, the kind of kid who would rather stay in and read Dostoevsky than do whatever everyone else was doing. Not much has changed.

I studied at Kabul Polytechnic, then SDSU, where I walked away from a computer science degree I never finished. Banking, fintech, and two years at a tech company you'd recognize came after that. Now I work as a home entertainment and automation technician, which mostly means I'm on my knees in somebody's living room calibrating a system that costs more than my car. You see a lot from down there. I write about that sometimes.

The rest of the time I read. Russian literature, classic American fiction, philosophy. Hemingway, Steinbeck, Camus, Dostoevsky. And I listen: the Beatles, who I will argue about with anyone, the qawwali my parents had around the house, whatever Joy Crookes is doing. I also support Real Madrid, which explains a lot about my emotional state on weekends.

This site is where I write. About books, about being an immigrant in America, about music, about family, and about the things I notice in rooms when nobody's paying attention to the guy running the cable.

If this ends up becoming a thing, i may turn it into other forms of media, if not, thats fine too, i just want to clean up my notes app coz im sick of the scribbles i keep finding everywhere, digital and physical, anyways, thanks for reading, appreciate any feedback you might have, good or bad, have a good life.

Feedback, suggestions, or requests: yayarodka@gmail.com

Love, Yaya
