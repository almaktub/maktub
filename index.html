mak·tub /mɑkˈtuːb/
adjective, Arabic. it is written. As in: fated. Destined. Already decided before you showed up. noun, Dari. a written document; a letter. Something set down so it isn't forgotten.

I picked this word because the only one I kept coming back to was document, document, document. For years I filled journals with half-sentences, scribbled memories, things that happened in my adult life that I didn't have the time to sit down and properly write. This is me sitting down. Slowly deciphering all of it. It turns out the Dari word for document is maktub. It also means fate. Make of that what you will.
